Apollo Music Player

Apollo is an open-source music player for Microsoft Windows, meticulously crafted using Python to deliver an exceptional audio playback and organization experience. It supports a wide array of audio formats, including MP3, AAC, M4A, MPC, OGG, FLAC, ALAC, APE, Opus, TAK, WavPack, WMA, WAV, MIDI, MOD, UMX, and XM, ensuring comprehensive compatibility for all your audio needs.

Apollo provides seamless audio playback with support for gapless playback, eliminating timing artifacts between consecutive tracks for an uninterrupted listening experience. It also features ReplayGain, which normalizes volume levels among individual tracks, ensuring a consistent and enjoyable listening experience across playlists.

Apollo excels in library management, allowing users to find, organize, and rename music based on audio tag values such as artist, album, track number, or other metadata. It can be configured to automatically monitor and manage select libraries while providing the option for manual control on a case-by-case basis.

Apollo stands out as a comprehensive, customizable, and user-friendly music player, designed to cater to all aspects of audio playback and library management on Microsoft Windows.
